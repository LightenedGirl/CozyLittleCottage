Mistakes That You Make Installing Solar Power System

May 13, 2026

Installing a solar power system can save a lot of money long-term, but some mistakes during planning or installation can reduce efficiency, damage equipment, or even create safety risks. Here are the most common mistakes people make.

A good solar setup is not just about buying panels — proper design, safety, and installation matter just as much.

1. Choosing the Wrong System Size

Many people install a system that is either:

  • Too small → doesn’t meet electricity needs
  • Too large → unnecessary investment

A proper load calculation should consider:

  • Daily energy usage (kWh)
  • Future appliances
  • Seasonal variations
  • Battery backup requirements

2. Ignoring Roof Direction and Shading

Solar panels perform best with maximum sunlight exposure.

Common mistakes:

  • Installing under shade from trees, water tanks, or nearby buildings
  • Wrong tilt angle
  • Wrong orientation
Helpful Tip

In India, south-facing panels usually perform best.

3. Using Low-Quality Components

Cheap components can reduce lifespan and efficiency.

  • Panels
  • Inverters
  • Batteries
  • Mounting structures
  • DC cables

A poor inverter or battery often fails long before the panels.

4. Poor Battery Planning

  • Undersized batteries drain quickly
  • Wrong battery type reduces backup time
  • No ventilation overheats batteries
Important

Battery bank sizing is critical

Always size the battery according to your real backup needs and usage pattern.

5. Wrong Wire Size

Undersized cables cause:

  • Voltage drop
  • Heating
  • Power loss
  • Fire hazards

DC solar wiring must be properly rated.

6. Skipping Proper Earthing and Lightning Protection

This is one of the most dangerous mistakes.

Without proper grounding:

  • Equipment can be damaged during lightning
  • Shock risk increases
  • Inverter failures become common
Safety Essentials

Every system should have earthing, surge protection, and a lightning arrestor — especially in Kerala’s monsoon climate.

7. Bad Mounting Structure

  • Rusting
  • Water leakage
  • Panels loosening in heavy wind

Aluminium or hot-dip galvanized structures are preferred.

8. No Space for Maintenance

Panels need periodic cleaning.

Mistakes include:

  • Installing panels too close together
  • No walkway access
  • Difficult inverter location

Dust and bird droppings can significantly reduce output.

9. Mixing Different Panels or Batteries

Using mismatched:

  • Solar panels
  • Battery capacities
  • Old and new batteries together

This causes imbalance and efficiency loss.

10. Ignoring Local Weather Conditions

Kerala Climate
  • Heavy rain
  • Humidity
  • Salt air near coastal regions
  • Strong winds

Proper waterproofing and corrosion protection are important.

11. Not Checking Net Metering Rules

For grid-tied systems, people sometimes:

  • Install before approval
  • Ignore utility regulations
  • Use non-approved inverters

This can delay connection.

12. Expecting Full Output All the Time

Solar production changes with:

  • Weather
  • Temperature
  • Dust
  • Season
  • Time of day
Reality Check

A “5 kW system” only produces 5 kW under ideal conditions.

13. DIY Installation Without Expertise

Solar systems involve:

  • High DC voltage
  • AC synchronization
  • Structural safety

Improper installation can damage appliances or create fire hazards.

14. Not Monitoring System Performance

Without monitoring:

  • Faults go unnoticed
  • Panel failures stay hidden
  • Energy losses increase

Modern inverters usually include apps for tracking output.

15. Focusing Only on Panel Wattage

People often compare only panel watts and ignore:

  • Inverter efficiency
  • Battery quality
  • Mounting quality
  • Cable losses
  • Installer expertise

System design matters more than just panel size.

A Good Solar Installation Should Include

  • Proper load analysis
  • Quality Tier-1 panels
  • Correct inverter sizing
  • Proper earthing
  • Surge protection
  • Good ventilation
  • Maintenance access
  • Monitoring system
  • Approved installer
