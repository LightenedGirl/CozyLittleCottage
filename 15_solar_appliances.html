Solar Water Heater

A solar water heater uses rooftop thermal collectors to heat water directly from sunlight — no electricity needed at all. For a Kerala home, this is one of the most practical solar investments you can make. A family of 4 can save ₹2,000–₹4,000 per year on electricity just from hot water alone.

  • Best capacity for a family of 4–5: 150–200 LPD
  • Top brands: Racold, V-Guard, Supreme Solar
  • Typical price: ₹15,000 – ₹40,000
  • Payback period: 3–5 years

For Kerala Homes

The most practical solar products for a Kerala home are, in order of impact: solar water heater (biggest electricity saving), solar garden & gate lights (zero running cost, no wiring), solar water pump if you have an open well, and solar lanterns for monsoon power cut backup. These four products together cost less than ₹30,000 and eliminate several recurring electricity expenses.

Before Buying

Always check wattage, warranty, and whether the product ships to your pincode. For larger items like water heaters, compare local vendors too — installation and after-sales service matter a lot for these products. Smaller gadgets like lanterns, fans, and garden lights are safe to buy online without worry.
