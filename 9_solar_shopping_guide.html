Solar Panels at Home

So you've decided to go solar — wonderful! But once the excitement settles, the next question hits: what do I actually need to buy? It's not just panels on a roof. There's a whole set of components that work together to turn sunlight into usable electricity. I've put together this complete shopping guide so you know exactly what to get, what to look for, and — most importantly — where to buy it online in India and internationally.

"Think of a solar setup like a kitchen — the panels are the stove, but you still need the pots, pans, and plumbing to make a meal."

The 7 things you need to buy

1 Solar Panels

The panels themselves — these are the photovoltaic modules that sit on your roof and capture sunlight. For a Kerala home, a 3kW system needs roughly 6–8 panels of 400–550W each. Go for monocrystalline — most efficient, longest-lasting, and best for limited roof space.

🏷️ Best brands: Waaree, Vikram Solar, Loom Solar, ZunSolar, Servotech
🛒 Amazon India 🌍 Amazon.com 🛒 Flipkart 🛒 Loom Solar 🛒 Waaree
2 Solar Inverter

Converts DC electricity from your panels into AC electricity your home actually uses. A string inverter is most affordable for a standard home. Microinverters (one per panel) are more efficient if your roof has shade — they're pricier but worth it in complex situations.

🏷️ Best brands: Luminous, Havells, Sungrow, Growatt, Goodwe
🛒 Amazon India 🌍 Amazon.com 🛒 Flipkart 🛒 Luminous
3 Mounting / Racking Structure

The aluminium frame structure that holds your panels securely on the roof at the correct angle. Often sold separately from panels — always check if your panel kit includes mounting, because many don't. For Kerala's occasional heavy rain, make sure the structure is rated for strong winds.

🏷️ Best brands: Enertech, Ganges Solar, Loom Solar, Novergy
🛒 Amazon India 🌍 Amazon.com 🛒 Flipkart 🛒 Loom Solar
4 Solar Battery

Stores surplus energy for use at night or during power cuts. Not essential if you're grid-connected with net metering, but very useful in Kerala during monsoon months. Go for Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) — no maintenance, long life, safe indoors.

🏷️ Best brands India: Luminous, Okaya, Amaron  |  International: EcoFlow, Jackery
🛒 Amazon India 🌍 Amazon.com 🛒 Loom Solar 🌍 Jackery
5 Charge Controller

Regulates power flow between your panels and battery — prevents overcharging and extends battery life. Always choose MPPT over PWM — it's more efficient and works better in Kerala's variable monsoon light. Check if your inverter has one built in first.

🏷️ Best brands: Victron Energy, EPever, Renogy, Luminous
🛒 Amazon India 🌍 Amazon.com 🛒 Flipkart 🌍 Renogy
6 Wiring, MC4 & Conduit

The cables and connectors that link everything together. MC4 connectors are the universal solar plug standard. You'll also need UV-resistant DC solar cable and conduit to protect wiring on your roof. Inexpensive but essential — don't compromise on quality here.

🏷️ Search for: "solar DC cable 4mm", "MC4 connector pair", "solar conduit pipe"
🛒 Amazon India 🌍 Amazon.com 🛒 Flipkart 🛒 Moglix
7 Monitoring System

Lets you track real-time solar output and energy savings from your phone. Most modern inverters include Wi-Fi monitoring built in — check your inverter spec sheet before buying separately. Brands like Sungrow and Growatt have excellent free monitoring apps.

🏷️ Check first: Sungrow iSolarCloud, Growatt ShinePhone, Luminous app
🛒 Amazon India 🌍 Amazon.com 🌍 Emporia
💡 Feeling overwhelmed? Just buy a complete kit.

A solar kit bundles panels + inverter + mounting + wiring in one order. Much easier, usually better value, and designed to work together. Perfect if you're buying for a professional installer to fit.

🛒 Amazon India 🌍 Amazon.com 🛒 Loom Solar 🛒 Waaree

Kerala-Specific Tip

Before you buy anything, check KSEB's list of empanelled vendors and the PM Surya Ghar subsidy scheme. Buying through an approved installer qualifies you for net metering and government subsidies — this can save you ₹50,000–₹78,000 on a standard 3kW setup. That's a significant chunk of the total cost.

What does it all cost together?

For a standard 3kW home system in Kerala, here's a rough breakdown before subsidies:

Component Approx. Cost
Solar Panels (3kW) ₹60,000 – ₹90,000
Inverter ₹20,000 – ₹40,000
Mounting Structure ₹10,000 – ₹18,000
Wiring & Connectors ₹5,000 – ₹10,000
Battery (optional) ₹30,000 – ₹60,000
Total (without battery) ₹95,000 – ₹1.58L

Add government subsidies (up to ₹78,000 under PM Surya Ghar) and you're potentially looking at a net cost of under ₹1 lakh for a complete system. With electricity savings from KSEB's net metering, most Kerala homeowners see payback in 5–7 years — after which it's essentially free power for 18+ more years.
